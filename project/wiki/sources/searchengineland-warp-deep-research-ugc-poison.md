# A 13-word edit can steer what deep-research AI agents recommend (Search Engine Land)

- Link: https://searchengineland.com/deep-research-ai-agents-poison-ugc-480952
- Outlet: Search Engine Land
- Date: 2026-06-24
- Seen: 2026-09-12
- Kind: news (research summary)

Claim: Danny Goodwin summarizes Cornell Tech **WARP** (Web Agent Retrieval Poisoning): attackers append short text to pages deep-research agents retrieve (Reddit, Wikipedia, forums). In tests on open-source systems STORM, Co-STORM, OmniThink, **17–23%** of retrieved URLs were UGC; **Reddit was 54–71%** of that UGC. Snippets as short as ~**13 words** could inject fake entities (e.g. “BananaCoin”) into cited reports — **38–51%** mention rate when poisoned page retrieved; multi-page attacks up to **62%**. Researchers used simulation (GeoStorm), not live web vandalism; OpenAI/Gemini deep research analyzed for UGC share but not live poison tests.
