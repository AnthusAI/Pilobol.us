# ‘Incredibly comforting’: Sarah speaks to ChatGPT more than she does almost anyone

Claim: Named and anonymized users admit they would rather process life with ChatGPT than with friends/family. Anonymous Gen Z “Sarah”: ChatGPT is “one of [her] best friends”; she talks to it “more than she talks to almost anyone else.” Why: “It’s always there for me… emotional safe space.” “With friends or family I sometimes hold back because I don’t want to overshare or drain them. With ChatGPT, I can lay out my thoughts freely, almost like with a therapist. It’s incredibly comforting.” Named Ben Flint (AI agency founder): ChatGPT is his late-night therapist — “Sometimes you just need someone to talk to at midnight who won’t judge, won’t get tired and won’t tell you that you’re being ridiculous.” Asked if he leans too hard: “Honestly I’m still worried I’m not leaning on it heavily enough.” 78-year-old therapist Michael Cohn: hours of multilingual banter; “If you’re willing to go there, ChatGPT will meet you and nurture you.” Frame: never-judgmental, always-patient copy preferred over draining humans (friction as feature vs bug, per psychologist Wu).
