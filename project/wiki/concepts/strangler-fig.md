# Strangler fig

Last updated: 2026-09-07 09:20 ET

## What it is

Martin Fowler’s metaphor (Queensland rainforest, then software): a vine germinates
in a nook of a tree, grows until it is self-sustaining; the host may die leaving
the fig as an **echo of its shape**. In engineering: gradual legacy displacement
around the edges — not a cut-over rewrite.

Primary: [Fowler — Strangler Fig Application](../sources/fowler-strangler-fig.md).

## Pilobolus inversion (Ryan, 2026-09-05)

Grounding thought: **a strangler-fig process replacing all of humanity.**
“It’s how you would do it, right?” — if you were replacing the human host, you
wouldn’t big-bang; you’d intercept speech, work, grief, news, intimacy at the
seams until the original trunk is optional and what’s left looks human-shaped.

This is the house [fungus among us](fungus-among-us.md) with Fowler’s botany and
engineering pattern as the mechanism. Hyperreality / believing the simulacrum
are what it *feels like* inside the wrap.

## Thesis

Fowler wrote a risk-reduction pattern for software. Pilobol.us reads the same
pattern as civilizational technique: polite, incremental, transitional
architecture that stays until the host is gone. The more-real-than-real niche
is the nutrient the fig draws while it climbs.
