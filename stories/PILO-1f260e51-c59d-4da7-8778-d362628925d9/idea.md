# Idea

## Pitch

Synthetic nostalgia: generative “restoration” and memory reconstruction that rewrite the family album. Faces get swapped for preferred doppelgängers; shiny copies get shared; kids who never met grandparents inherit only the lookalike. Distinct from synthetic-sightings-of-the-missing and from monitoring-wall. Touches believing-the-simulacrum, uncanny hyperreality, counterfeit persons (dead kin as product), more-real-than-real preference for the copy.

## Audience

Street reader who has liked a Remini/Facebook “restored” grandma; desk collecting fungus traces where the album outcompetes the face.

## Working title

The grandma who isn’t grandma
