Claim (section “Beyond Memorization: Attribute inference for text”): Citing the Beyond Memorization research, sensitive leakage from free-form text is not only verbatim memorization — models accurately infer attributes such as age, gender, education, occupation, location, place of birth, and income/economic status. Offhand disclosures (e.g. marital status in Slack) become profile fields that can be recorded, learned, or repeated. Ryan flagged this as the relevant seam for the WSJ/practical-obscurity haul: attribute inference is the extract step before search-and-match deanonymization.
