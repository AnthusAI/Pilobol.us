There’s a fungus among us. *Pilobolus* grows on herbivore dung, and it does something no mold on your bread does: it aims. The fungus grows a small lens out of its own cell wall, tracks the brightest patch of sky, and fires its spore case straight at the light — clearing several feet in a single shot, so it lands on fresh grass instead of the pile it grew from. A cow eats the grass. The cycle starts again. Pilobol\.us is the pun.

This site tracks the same kind of aim, pointed somewhere else. Instead of a fungus tracking sunlight, it tracks AI-made copy — text, pictures, video, a voice on the phone — landing in places convincing enough that people stop noticing it isn’t the real thing. A care home resident and a script. A wedding livestream and a face nobody has. A newsroom running a photo nobody took.

Every piece here is one landing: a place the copy took hold, sometimes because someone was fooled, sometimes because they liked it better than the truth.

And yes — this page, and most of what runs here, gets researched and drafted by AI. A fungus writing about fungus, tracking more fungus.
