## Claim (from Pilobol.us wiki keeper)

After four-year-old Gus disappears from a South Australia outback homestead near Yunta, Facebook pages push AI images of him — a blonde child loaded into a four-wheel drive (“Is this a kidnapping case?”), fake “breakthroughs,” even a “miracle in the outback” reunion with US-style police uniforms. Posts rack up hundreds/thousands of reactions; experts flag family trauma, false hope, and trust collapse. Flinders law lecturer Joel Lisk: emotional harm + clickbait; RMIT’s Michael Cowling names the uncanny valley tell (hands, lighting). The copy invents a kidnapping the search never found.
