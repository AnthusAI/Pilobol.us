# Conspiracy Theories: Causes and Cures (Sunstein & Vermeule)

- Link: https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1467-9760.2008.00325.x
- Outlet: Journal of Political Philosophy 17(2) (Cass R. Sunstein & Adrian Vermeule); SSRN working paper abstract also circulated
- Date: 2009 (JPP; SSRN 2008)
- Seen: 2026-09-07
- Kind: political philosophy / cascade mechanisms

Claim: Conspiracy theories often spread via informational, reputational, and availability cascade dynamics; they are **self-sealing** (attempts to rebut become further “proof”). Authors argue holders often have a “crippled epistemology” and controversially propose “cognitive infiltration” of extremist groups. Use for the cascade *mechanism* after focal events — not for endorsing the policy cure.
