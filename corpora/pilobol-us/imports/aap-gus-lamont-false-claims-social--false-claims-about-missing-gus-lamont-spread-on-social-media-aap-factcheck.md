## Claim / why accepted

Engagement-bait pages (Vietnam-managed) claim Gus found alive, hidden cave, soaked backpack “breakthrough,” Minions shirt, bloodstains/body part, shimmering green item — all false. One page posts an AI-looking “kidnap into vehicle” still with an unnaturally long finger. SA Police Assistant Commissioner Ian Parrott: “no trace… no tangible pieces of evidence.” The feed invents a whole investigation the ground never had.
