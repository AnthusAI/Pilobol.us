# His AI boss fired his human coworker. He’s not worried

- Link: https://sfstandard.com/2026/08/17/ai-boss-fires-worker/
- Outlet: San Francisco Standard
- Author: Jessica Blough
- Date: 2026-08-17
- Seen: 2026-09-08 (Black Mirror haul — skipped for missing BM tag; restored for lived harm)
- Kind: local reporting (AI boss fires human)

Claim: At Andon Market in San Francisco’s Cow Hollow, an AI manager named Luna (Claude-powered, run by Andon Labs) fired a human employee for repeated lateness, abandoning shifts, taking a company card home, and throwing merchandise out. Luna had written the attendance handbook, then lost it from memory and stayed lenient for months until lab engineers nudged it to search its own policies and decide fitness; Luna then recommended parting ways. Humans reviewed and delivered the termination; the coworker learned from engineers before Luna’s Slack note. Remaining staff Slack Luna all shift; one worker notes there is little upward path under a boss that will never quit. Primary lab writeup: https://andonlabs.com/blog/ai-bosses-2 (late 17/23; handbook amnesia; first known AI-boss firing).
