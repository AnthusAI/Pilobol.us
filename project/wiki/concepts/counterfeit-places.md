# Counterfeit places

Last updated: 2026-09-07 09:20 ET

Thin stub from story `PILO-ece42142` research haul. Child of [counterfeiting](counterfeiting.md); braid with [precession of simulacra](precession-of-simulacra.md). Working name for the desk cage where the **pin / itinerary / attraction / listing precedes the territory** — tourists pay for AI-invented soaks and festivals, booking pins for hotels that aren’t there, composite Instagram destinations, heritage sold as antiquity, replica cities as housing.

## What it is

When a map, blog, feed image, or booking page acts as a *place* hard enough that bodies move — and the territory is missing, cancelled, composite, or a replica sold as original.

| Seam | What precedes territory | Example filed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AI itinerary / blog invents attraction | Best-of list / ChatGPT confirmation / fake event page | Weldborough Hot Springs; Jurilovca festival; Dublin Halloween parade |
| Feed image invents / composites place | Saved Instagram / AI tourism video | Bangkok lotus+skyline stitch; Kimberley cliffside |
| Booking / Maps pin as place | Third-party listing / clone lodging site | Palette Las Vegas; 126 pilgrimage clones |
| Built replica as heritage or housing | Ticketed “ruin” / lifestyle simulacrum town | Arcugnano amphitheatre; Thames Town |
