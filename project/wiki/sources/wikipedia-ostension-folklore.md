# Ostension (folklore) — legend enacted / misread as reality

- Link: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ostension_(folklore)
- Outlet: Wikipedia (surveying Dégh & Vázsonyi 1983; Ellis; Eco; Tolbert on reverse ostension)
- Date: page consulted 2026-09-07 (seminal paper: Dégh & Vázsonyi, *Journal of Folklore Research* 20, 1983)
- Seen: 2026-09-07
- Kind: folklore encyclopedia (contemporary legend)

Claim: **Ostension** = real-life happenings shaped by a pre-existing legend. Pure ostension acts the legend out; **pseudo-ostension** fabricates evidence/hoaxes to look like the legend; **quasi-ostension** misreads ambiguous evidence *as* the legend (many media panics). Dégh/Vázsonyi: “fact can become narrative and narrative can become fact.” Reverse ostension (Tolbert): invent folklore-like narrative where none existed (Slender Man). Folklore twin of competing post-event stories and synthetic “sightings.”
