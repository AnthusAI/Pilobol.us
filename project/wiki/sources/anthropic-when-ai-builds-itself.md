# When AI builds itself

- Link: https://www.anthropic.com/institute/recursive-self-improvement
- Outlet: Anthropic Institute
- Author: Marina Favaro, Jack Clark (eds. Santi Ruiz)
- Date: 2026-06-06
- Seen: 2026-09-07 (monitoring-wall haul)
- Kind: primary lab essay (RSI / AI writing AI)

Claim: Anthropic reports that as of May 2026 more than 80% of code merged into its production codebase was authored by Claude (up from low single digits before Claude Code, Feb 2025). Q2 2026 engineers merged ~8× as many lines/day as in 2024; one quoted engineer had written no code himself for ~5 months. Claude’s open-ended task success hit 76% (May 2026); a fixed training-speedup loop jumped ~3× (Opus 4, May 2025) to ~52× (Mythos Preview, Apr 2026). Agents recovered 97% of a weak→strong supervision research gap vs ~23% for two humans in a week. Essay says full recursive self-improvement is not here yet — and argues for verifiable global pause options before institutions are ready.
