# Idea

## Pitch (Ryan lock 2026-09-09)

Pilobolus spine is **dehumanization of the workers**, not privacy harm to privileged glasses customers.

Known baseline: Google and Apple already run private photos and mail through ML models. People mostly accept that machines see the inbox.

Pilobolus cut: Meta behaves as if human labelers at Sama (Nairobi) are the same category of thing — cheap models you feed intimate frames into — so revealing a bedroom to a person does not count as revealing it to a person. Those workers sit on the corporation’s **worker** side of the world, not the **customer** side, so they do not really matter in the privacy ledger. Humans processed as models.

Lived specimen still: Ray-Ban Meta share → annotators label bathrooms / undressing / sex / bank cards → after Swedish papers, contract ends → ~1,108 redundancies. Distinct from street “pervert glasses” / NameTag keepers. Culture already tagged the footage side “Black Mirror”; house take is the category error that treats people like weights.

Touches: fungus-among-us / strangler-fig (human labor hollowed into model-shaped slots), black-mirror-unintended (secondary), institutional-lag.

## Audience

Street reader who can feel “the company already sorted these humans out of personhood.”

## Working title (provisional)

Someone else is wearing your eyes — or retitle toward the workers-as-models beat when drafting resumes.

## Explicitly not the story

Not a privacy explainer for Meta customers. Not “private means not seen by anyone who matters” as the main thesis. Customers stay background.
