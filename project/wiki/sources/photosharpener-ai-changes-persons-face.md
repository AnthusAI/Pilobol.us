# How do I restore an old family photo when AI keeps changing the person's face? (PhotoSharpener)

- Link: https://photosharpener.com/blog/how-do-i-restore-an-old-family-photo-when-ai-keeps-changing-the-persons-face/

- Date: 2026-04-22
- Seen: 2026-09-08
- Kind: mechanism card (identity drift)

Claim: Shoebox grandma scan → AI “restore” → cleaner photo whose face belongs to a stranger — “most common complaint in every forum” on AI photo restoration. Tools generate statistically likely faces, not recovered originals; beautification bundled with damage repair accelerates **identity drift**. Worse when tears/scratches erase structure — reconstruction, not restoration. Modular advice: damage pass first, optional GFPGAN later; check eyes/nose/jaw/skin at 100% zoom. When major facial detail is gone, AI yields a plausible generated face with “no reliable way to tell how much… reflects the original.” Human restorer + reference photos as accountable alternative.
