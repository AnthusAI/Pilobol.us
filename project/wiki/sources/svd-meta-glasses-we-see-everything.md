# Meta’s AI Smart Glasses — Workers Say “We See Everything” (SvD / GP)

- Link: https://www.svd.se/a/K8nrV4/metas-ai-smart-glasses-and-data-privacy-concerns-workers-say-we-see-everything
- Outlet: Svenska Dagbladet (with Göteborgs-Posten; Nairobi reporter Naipanoi Lepapa)
- Date: ~late Feb 2026 (investigation; English page live)
- Seen: 2026-09-09
- Kind: investigative primary (Black Mirror unintended / lived harm)

Claim: Joint SvD/GP investigation interviews unnamed Sama annotators in Nairobi who label Meta Ray-Ban AI glasses media. Workers describe bathroom visits, undressing, sex filmed while wearing the glasses, bank cards, porn-watching, and a bedside-table clip where a wife changes clothes after the wearer leaves the room. Quote spine: “We see everything — from living rooms to naked bodies.” Blur filters sometimes fail. Swedish shop staff reassure buyers that “nothing is shared”; reporters’ own pair still phones Meta servers for live AI. Terms admit human review of AI interactions. Engine-room scene: confidential hotel interviews; workers fear firing if they question the stream.
