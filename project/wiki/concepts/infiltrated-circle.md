# Infiltrated discussion circle

Last updated: 2026-09-05 17:05 ET

## What it is

Yuval Noah Harari’s framing (as briefed for this desk): the digital public
sphere is like a human discussion circle infiltrated by robots. Once you cannot
tell who is real, trust in the conversation itself collapses — not merely trust
in this or that speaker.

Pilobol.us takes that as a standing editorial frame. The robots are not a side
cast; they are the infection inside the circle. See also
[fungus among us](fungus-among-us.md) and [simulacrum](simulacrum.md).

## Thesis

Collapse of conversational trust is the story, not “AI got smarter.” The damage
is social epistemology: shared speech stops being a reliable path to shared
reality. Pieces should show *how* infiltration works in a concrete arena
(platform, genre, institution), what tells still exist, and what happens when
those tells fail.

Do not flatten Harari into a mascot. Use the circle metaphor when it sharpens
the piece; cite and verify the specific Harari passage before reader-facing
quotation beyond Ryan’s briefed line.
