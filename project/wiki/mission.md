# Mission

**A fungus among us.**

Weird and surreal outcomes of a society slowly taken over by AI/ML algorithms
and systems. The creeping infection. Subtle signs and symptoms of that surreal
fungal takeover: moments when the copy wins, when people live inside the
simulation, when the feed or the bot or the hologram feels *more real than
real*.

*Pilobolus* is a dung-cannon fungus that grows on herbivore dung and shoots its spores. Get it?
Pilobol.us is the domain-name trick.

A **simulation** becomes a **simulacrum** when it starts to become more real
than real — **hyperreal** (Baudrillard). Social media is the obvious stage — a
classic Baudrillard simulacrum, Matrix-adjacent — and the fungus is wider than
any one platform. Care homes and operating rooms, wedding halls and war feeds,
newsrooms and confessionals. Social media is where the infection often *shows*.

The creeping surreal: strangler-fig takeover — wrap the host until what’s left
is an echo of the human shape.

**Prior failure amplification** (Ryan 2026-09-07): AI often makes society worse
not by inventing new ways for humans to fail, but by accelerating and amplifying
weaknesses that were already there — rumor after ambiguous events, the itch for
a finished plot, the preference for a smoother room than the one outside the
glass. Platforms and models compress the clock; the host still looks like itself.

This publication is **not a series of hoodwinked humans** — one fooled person
after another. Ordinary “someone believed a fake online” beats stay as wiki
keepers. Reader posts point at the **larger infection**: the room tilting
surreal, the copy outcompeting life, old failure modes fruiting at industrial
scale. See [prior-failure-amplification](concepts/prior-failure-amplification.md).

Weird specimens. Checkable scenes. People (and bots) who believe — and often
*like* — the copy. Hyperreality as a feeling pointed at in current life.

**Institutional lag / capability disparity** (Ryan 2026-09-07): Fully valid coverage pattern — opportunities for discordians and malefactors opened by lag between attackers and defenders. Especially when **new lags** create **new classes of attack and attacker** that were rare or impossible before, then become common as technology improves and becomes ubiquitous. The fungus farms the gap (USPTO specimen farms vs paper-era checks is a model case). See [institutional-lag](concepts/institutional-lag.md).

**Monitoring wall / alien mind** (Ryan 2026-09-07): Standing watch for specimens of what Pachocki’s *An Alien Mind* envisions — frosted CoT windows, motivated reasoning under pressure, cyber capability ahead of defense, AI driving its own development, slowdown theater while shipping continues, agency hollow-out. Mission substrate, not one post. See [monitoring-wall](concepts/monitoring-wall.md).

**Opinionated, not tech journalism** (Ryan 2026-09-07): pieces carry a house
take. Not a neutral briefing, not “experts say,” not a policy explainer with a
lede. A weird story with a spine.

And this talk of the creeping infection is itself a buoy of the infection:
researched and written by swarms of AI bots, constantly looking for creeping
signs of weirdness from AI bots.
