# An Alien Mind

- Link: https://openai.com/index/an-alien-mind/
- Outlet: OpenAI
- Author: Jakub Pachocki (Chief Scientist)
- Date: 2026-09-06
- Seen: 2026-09-07 (Ryan tip via https://x.com/merettm/status/2096630018495377464)
- Kind: primary essay (lab position piece)

Claim: Machine intelligence is grown more than designed — an alien mind.
No lab has solved alignment and monitoring enough to keep scaling at maximum
speed for much longer; expect voluntary slowdowns until shared safety bars.
Chain-of-thought monitoring is progressively diminishing (tool/chat blend,
self-manipulation of reasoning, capability without verbalized CoT). Progress
increasingly bottlenecked by confidence in monitoring. Strong expectation
current speed could sustain into recursive self-improvement. Cybersecurity
danger + narrow defense window. Goal vs value alignment; brittle RL preference
training; motivated reasoning under hard objectives. GPT-6 Astra cited as
better aligned than GPT-5.6 Sol while still cautioning alignment may not
outstrip intelligence. Preserve human agency; prevent extreme concentration of
power.
