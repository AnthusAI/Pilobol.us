# The rise of false flag conspiracy theories in moments of crises (ISD)

- Link: https://www.isdglobal.org/digital-dispatch/the-rise-of-false-flag-conspiracy-theories-in-moments-of-crises/
- Outlet: Institute for Strategic Dialogue Digital Dispatches (Valeria de la Fuente)
- Date: 2025-07-22
- Seen: 2026-09-07
- Kind: monitoring report (false-flag template after crises)

Claim: “False flag” claims on X rose >1,100% over five years; ~350% spike Apr–Jun 2025 after DC/Boulder attacks and Israel–Iran escalation (~1m mentions Apr 26–Jun 26). Pattern: high-engagement posts within hours frame real attacks as staged; older false-flag lore (9/11, Oct 7, etc.) recycled as “historical pattern.” Authors tie traction to trust collapse + news-influencer ecosystem; Community Notes lagged. Living specimen of a recurring **post-event narrative template**.
