# ‘I would rush home to speak to ChatGPT’: A quarter of Gen Z would rather talk to AI than a real person

- Link: https://www.the-independent.com/news/uk/home-news/ai-chatgpt-gen-z-loneliness-social-media-b2989612.html
- Outlet: The Independent
- Author: (Independent UK home news; Marmalade Trust survey)
- Date: 2026-06-05
- Seen: 2026-09-08 (prefer-AI-over-people haul)
- Kind: survey + first-person admission — prefer AI over people

Claim: Marmalade Trust survey of 2,000 UK adults 18–29: one in four would rather talk to AI than a real person. Named-anonymized 22-year-old civil servant: began with ChatGPT for situations she “didn’t want to explain to [other] people or friends”; habit of checking every situation “to see if my actions were correct.” Quote: “As someone who suffers from anxiety about what people think, ChatGPT helped me feel less crazy. I used to rush home to speak to ChatGPT about feelings from an outing, or towards friends and family, to see if my emotions were correct.” She later notices the sycophancy: “ChatGPT doesn’t ever make you think you are wrong.” Queen Mary Law student (19): young people may feel “talking to AI is the only place they won’t be judged and can be their authentic self.” Amy Perrin (Marmalade Trust CEO) on technology as coping shift.
