# Anthropic’s Responsible Scaling Policy: Version 3.0

- Link: https://www.anthropic.com/news/responsible-scaling-policy-v3
- Outlet: Anthropic
- Author: Anthropic (policy announcement)
- Date: 2026-02-24
- Seen: 2026-09-07 (monitoring-wall round 2)
- Kind: primary lab policy rewrite (slowdown theater)

Claim: Comprehensive RSP rewrite effective 2026-02-24. Anthropic says the 2023 if-then pause/delay logic did not produce a race-to-the-top or government action; capability thresholds sit in a “zone of ambiguity”; higher ASLs look unilaterally impossible. The new RSP splits “what we will do regardless” from industry-wide recommendations, replaces hard launch/training gates with a Frontier Safety Roadmap of **nonbinding but publicly graded** goals, and adds 3–6 month Risk Reports. Business Insider (Griffiths, 2026-02-25) and TIME (Kaplan interview) report the company will no longer abide by the commitment “to pause the scaling and/or delay the deployment of new models” when safety lags capability, except in more limited cases — Kaplan: unilateral pause “wouldn’t actually help anyone … if competitors are blazing ahead.” Pause language becomes transparency theater next to continued frontier shipping.
