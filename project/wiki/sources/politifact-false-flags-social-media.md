# False flags: real ops vs social-media template (PolitiFact)

- Link: https://www.politifact.com/article/2022/02/08/false-flags-theyre-real-far-less-widespread-social/
- Outlet: PolitiFact (Bill McCarthy & Louis Jacobson; historians Olmsted, Silbey, Radnitz, Starbird)
- Date: 2022-02-08
- Seen: 2026-09-07
- Kind: explanatory journalism (theory of the template)

Claim: Confirmed historical false-flag *operations* exist (e.g. Gleiwitz 1939, Mukden 1931) but are outnumbered by baseless claims that *real* crises were staged. After 9/11 and Iraq WMD deception, “false flag” became a reusable interpretive frame (Sandy Hook “crisis actors,” Jan. 6, school shootings). Olmsted: proven government lies feed attraction; Radnitz: intuition that politicians exploit crises makes the frame plausible; Starbird: confusion between real threat and rumor is sometimes the point. Desk bridge: template ≠ history of ops (Gleiwitz already filed).
