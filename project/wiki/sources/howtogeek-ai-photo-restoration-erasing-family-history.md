# AI photo restoration is erasing your family's real history (How-To Geek)

- Link: https://www.howtogeek.com/ai-photo-restoration-is-erasing-your-familys-real-history/
- Outlet: How-To Geek
- Date: ~2026 (live page; Justin Duino byline credit on piece)
- Seen: 2026-09-08
- Kind: first-person specimen (surreal)

Claim: Author’s parents gleefully “restore” damaged/faded family photos via ChatGPT and post the shiny versions on Facebook for nostalgia likes. Generative tools do not restore — they invent a new photo from the input. Library of Congress Pie Town family demo: AI extends the frame, changes wall posters, and replaces faces with lookalikes (“hired doppelgängers”). Dad’s AI-milled self-portrait is “obviously (and disturbingly) not his face.” Pre-birth dad photos: author cannot tell which face was real without side-by-side. Never met grandfathers — photos were the only evidence; voluntary replacement with creepy doppelgängers in places that didn’t exist. Warns children may never know what grandparents actually looked like.
