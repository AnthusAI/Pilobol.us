# Idea

## Pitch

**Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)** and its cousin **AEO** (“answer engine optimization”) are what PR and marketing agencies now sell openly: not just ranking blue links, but **being cited** when ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, or Google AI Mode synthesize an answer. Earned media, Reddit threads, statistics, and “expert quotes” become **retrieval bait** for an oracle the reader treats as research.

Parallel infection: brands and vendors **seed user-generated platforms** (especially Reddit) with covert promo so LLMs scrape friendly mentions into answers — while volunteer moderators discover they are guarding training fodder for the next citation. Institutional lag: platform spam LLMs vs. marketers optimizing for citation vs. users who added “reddit” to escape SEO slop.

Touches **subtle steering**, **infiltrated discussion circle** (the answer sounds like a room of peers), **simulacrum / hyperreality** (third-party credibility without a third party), **strangler-fig** (search still looks like help; the fruiting body is the cited paragraph).

## Audience

Street reader who asks an AI “what’s the best ___?” and trusts the footnoted paragraph; desk tracking agency pitch copy, mod bans, and retrieval-poisoning research.
