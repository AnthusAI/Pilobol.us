# Idea — Strangler fig / fungus wrapping humanity

## Hook
Martin Fowler’s **Strangler Fig Application** (gradual wrap of a legacy system
until the host is optional) inverted: that is what the fungus is doing to human
society — platforms, feeds, assistants wrapping life until the original shape
is only an echo.

Primary receipt: https://martinfowler.com/bliki/StranglerFigApplication.html

## Pilobolus lock
- Not a tech-journalism explainer of enterprise migration patterns.
- Weird story: one person noticing their life is being wrapped (routes, replies,
  choices arriving pre-chewed) until they can’t find the host underneath.
- Mission DNA already has this metaphor (strangler-fig / fungus among us).
- Name the thing early in plain English; one example; street-readable.
- No house self-name in story prose; no “not A, it’s B” stacks.

## Shape (pick when drafting)
Prefer inside-user or morning-after: they still *like* the wrap, then the uncanny
when they try to go around it.

## Out of scope
Enterprise consulting how-to; Fowler biography; multi-specimen braid.
