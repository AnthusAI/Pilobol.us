Claim: Writer tracks screen for 16 days and finds he has not written a paragraph without AI in three months — he asked the tools to draft, research, manage thinking; they granted speed. Inversion: early 2025 think→outline→AI research→write himself; by March 2026 tell the agent what to think → multi-agent critics rewrite → he only approves. Sharp bite: needing to write his own employment contract terms (equity, bonuses, KPIs) — work he “knows cold” — he opens the chat anyway; “It simply didn’t occur to me to try.” Blank page isn’t the problem; writing ceased to be a step. Self-described iatrogenesis: the helper he wished for erodes the capacity displacement had taught him to keep. Essay about AI dependency produced through the same pipeline.
