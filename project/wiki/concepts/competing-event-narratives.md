# Competing event narratives

Last updated: 2026-09-07

Cage: After a **focal public event** (disaster, attack, disappearance, scandal),
many incompatible stories form, recruit believers, and harden into parallel
publics — rumor chains, disaster myths, conspiracy cascades, “false flag”
templates, framing contests. Generative AI / synthetic sightings are a
**sped-up fruiting body** of this older pattern, not the first mushroom.

Child/sibling of [information-habit](information-habit.md) and
[synthetic-sightings](synthetic-sightings.md); braids
[historical-lineage](historical-lineage.md), [liars-dividend](liars-dividend.md),
[infiltrated-circle](infiltrated-circle.md), [state-made-belief](state-made-belief.md),
[fungus-among-us](fungus-among-us.md).

## What it is

Not one lie vs the truth. A **proliferation ecology**: importance × ambiguity
(Allport/Postman) seeds rumor; informational and availability cascades make
some versions snowball; disaster sociology catalogs sticky false templates
(panic/looting myths); folklore names ostension (legend misread or staged as
fact); crisis politics names framing contests / crisis exploitation; digital
culture names ideological entrepreneurs who sell one reading of every crisis.

## Thesis

The uncanny AI move (fake kidnap still mid-search) inherits a natural law of
public events: when stakes are high and evidence is incomplete, **many
narratives are the default**, not the exception. Platforms and models compress
the clock; the underlying research lanes already had names.
