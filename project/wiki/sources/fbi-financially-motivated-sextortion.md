# Financially motivated sextortion — FBI / FinCEN figures

- Link: https://www.fbi.gov/how-we-can-help-you/common-frauds-and-scams/sextortion/financially-motivated-sextortion
- Outlet: FBI; FinCEN notice (2025-09)
- Date: ongoing (figures seen 2026-09-09)
- Seen: 2026-09-09
- Kind: official statistics

Claim: Extortion-for-money is already an industrialized business run by humans. The FBI logged roughly 55,000 sextortion and extortion reports in a year with $33.5M in losses, a 59% jump in report volume over 2023. Demands are deliberately small: FinCEN records payments from minors typically $10–50 and from adults $500–2,500 — sized to be paid rather than reported. Offenders are usually overseas, primarily West Africa and Southeast Asia. The FBI is aware of more than 20 suicides tied to these schemes.

House value: the proof that the extortion economy does not need inventing — it exists at scale, run by people, and the expensive remaining step is finding targets worth approaching. Use as closing scale, never as a protagonist.
