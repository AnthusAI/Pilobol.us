# Think Your Anonymous Account Is Safe? AI Might Figure Out Who You Are (WSJ)

- Link: https://www.wsj.com/tech/ai/ai-anonymous-accounts-6dfd6d2e
- Outlet: The Wall Street Journal
- Date: ~2026 (WSJ tech/AI)
- Seen: 2026-09-09
- Kind: news (Ryan tip)

Claim: Large language models can unmask pseudonymous social-media profiles by piecing together scattered public clues and matching them to other online accounts. WSJ frames the old joke (“nobody knows you’re a dog”) as failing. Cites the Lermen/Paleka et al. study; one described test strips identifiers from Hacker News users who had LinkedIn in bio and asks AI to recover the person from public information.
