## Formal terms (quick defs)

1. **Rumor (Allport/Postman)** — unverified belief-proposition whose volume ≈ importance × ambiguity.
2. **Informational cascade** — rational imitation of others’ actions while ignoring private info; brittle.
3. **Availability cascade** — belief gains plausibility as it becomes more available in discourse (info + reputation).
4. **Conspiracy cascade** — self-sealing conspiracy beliefs spreading via those cascade channels.
5. **Disaster myths** — empirically false but sticky templates about post-disaster behavior (panic, looting…).
6. **Framing contests / crisis exploitation** — political battles to define a crisis’s meaning, blame, and lessons.
7. **Ostension** — living out / faking / misreading events through a pre-existing legend.
8. **Ideological (availability) entrepreneurs** — actors who seed and sell cascades/ideologies after upheaval.
9. **False-flag narrative template** — recurring claim that a real crisis was staged for ulterior ends.
