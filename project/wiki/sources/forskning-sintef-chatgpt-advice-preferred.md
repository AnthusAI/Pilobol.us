# Mental health study: young people prefer ChatGPT’s advice over professionals’ (SINTEF)

- Link: https://www.forskning.no/barn-og-ungdom-helsetjenester-informasjonsteknologi/mental-helse-unge-far-bedre-svar-av-chatgpt-enn-av-fagfolk/2645803
- Companion (EN summary via MedicalXpress/search): Digital Health DOI 10.1177/20552076261427447
- Outlet: forskning.no (Georg Mathisen / SINTEF)
- Author: Marita Skjuve, Asbjørn Følstad, Petter Bae Brandtzæg (study)
- Date: 2026-04-09 (forskning.no); study Digital Health Feb 2026
- Seen: 2026-09-08 (prefer-AI-over-people haul)
- Kind: blind preference study — ChatGPT answers preferred to human pros

Claim: Blind evaluation of answers to real youth mental-health questions (ung.no): ChatGPT (GPT-4) vs health professionals. n≈123 young people + 31 professionals. Young people gave ChatGPT the best grades throughout; rated higher for relevance and utility; preferred clear, actionable answers. Skjuve: young people like ChatGPT “a little better because they are easy to understand and are perceived as being immediately useful.” Professionals also scored ChatGPT well but less emphatically / more critical on empathy/detail. Follow-up (not yet published in this write-up): when source is labeled, preference shifts back toward humans — preference for the copy when the mask is on. Distinct from Sentio stay-off (different survey/outlet).
