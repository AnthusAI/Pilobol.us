Claim: Immediate post-Dwarkesh amplification of the OpenAI exit as courage-narrative. Aschenbrenner (Columbia at 19; Superalignment) says he wrote a memo after a “major security incident” that OpenAI security was “egregiously insufficient” against theft of “key algorithmic secrets from foreign actors.” HR warned the CCP-espionage worry was “racist” and “unconstructive”; lawyer probed whether he and the team were “loyal to the company.” Fired for alleged leak of a “brainstorming document on preparedness… for AGI” shared with three external researchers — including a line about “planning for AGI by 2027 to 2028.” He: sharing was “totally normal”; planning horizon “the sort of thing Sam says publicly all the time.” OpenAI: concerns “did not lead to his separation”; “disagree with many of the claims he has since made.” This package — fired for seeing the danger too clearly — is the origin myth that made Situational Awareness feel prophetic rather than merely opinionated.
