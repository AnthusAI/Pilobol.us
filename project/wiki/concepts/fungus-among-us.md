# Fungus among us

Last updated: 2026-09-07 (monitoring-wall watch)

## What it is

The house metaphor for Pilobol.us: synthetic and automated speakers as a
creeping fungus in human society — especially in the digital public sphere.
Mycelium is distributed, often invisible at the tips, and structural once it
has colonized the host. So are botnets, engagement farms, persona mills, and
fluent models speaking in the gaps where a person used to be.

The title beat — *a fungus among us* — is the publication’s through-line, not
a single essay topic. Pair with the
[infiltrated discussion circle](infiltrated-circle.md) (what the host feels)
and [simulacrum](simulacrum.md) (what the speech has become).

## Thesis

Infection language beats “disruption” language. Fungus names spread, latency,
and host dependency without requiring a single villain. The story is rarely one
model release; it is colonization of the channels people use to check what is
true together.

Use the metaphor when it clarifies mechanism (hyphae into a feed, fruiting as
viral posts, spores as copy-paste personas). Drop it when it becomes costume.
