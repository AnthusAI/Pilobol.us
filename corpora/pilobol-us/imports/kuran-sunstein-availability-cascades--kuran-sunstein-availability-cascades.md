# Availability Cascades and Risk Regulation (Kuran & Sunstein)

Source: https://sites.duke.edu/timurkuran/files/2016/10/availability-cascades-1.original.pdf

## Claim (from Pilobol.us wiki keeper)

An **availability cascade** is a self-reinforcing process of collective belief: an expressed perception triggers a chain reaction that makes the perception more plausible as it becomes more available in public discourse. Combines informational cascades (learn from others’ apparent beliefs) and reputational cascades (endorse to keep social acceptance). **Availability entrepreneurs** try to trigger cascades that advance their agendas (Love Canal, Alar, TWA 800 as cases). Names the snowball of “this reading of the event is everywhere, so it must be true.”
