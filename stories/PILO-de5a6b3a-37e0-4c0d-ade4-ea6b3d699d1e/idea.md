# Idea — The project arrived with a manager

## Pitch

In May 2026, Zhicheng Han reported that Get Shit Done’s Codex new-project
workflow had written its generated planning sections into a tracked
`CLAUDE.md`, though the workflow said Codex should use `AGENTS.md`. The command
finished cleanly. The fresh project now held a handbook from the workflow in a
document later AI helpers could treat as its local instructions.

This is one documented, ordinary software scene in which a tool’s habits
crossed into a new workspace and gained the voice of house rules. It is a
small, repaired bug. Its unease comes from how helpful and quiet it was.

## Narrative / presentation intent

Over one shoulder: Han in Hannover, Germany, files a detailed public issue
after the final step of project creation says it updated the wrong document.
The computer’s “updated” result is the hinge. Explain the two files in ordinary
terms and return to the blank project with instructions already on its wall.
