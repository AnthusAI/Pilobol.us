# Conspiracies, Ideological Entrepreneurs, and Digital Popular Culture (Hyzen & Van den Bulck)

- Link: https://www.cogitatiopress.com/mediaandcommunication/article/viewFile/4092/2268
- Outlet: Media and Communication 9(3) (Aaron Hyzen & Hilde Van den Bulck); DOI 10.17645/mac.v9i3.4092
- Date: 2021-09-13
- Seen: 2026-09-07
- Kind: media/cultural studies (conspiracy mainstreaming)

Claim: Conspiracy theories move margins→mainstream via **ideological entrepreneurs** (North/Storr lineage) who sell alternative world-explanations in upheaval, using digital-media and popular-culture affordances plus fandom-like audiences. Cases: Alex Jones / Infowars and QAnon. Complements Kuran/Sunstein’s “availability entrepreneurs” with a propaganda + celebrity + participatory-culture frame for who *seeds* competing event narratives.
