Child of [counterfeiting](counterfeiting.md); specimen lane for

## What it is

When a bot or synthetic persona stops being a labeled tool and starts acting as
a **person in the room** — journalist, Wikipedia editor, monk, CEO on Zoom,
classmate/partner, local on X, pundit byline, support rep — so that peers treat
the copy as a speaker.

Sister cage to [believe in the rainbow](believe-in-the-rainbow.md) (belief
substrate / money-trust): here the forged thing is the *speaker*, not the note.

## Thesis

Damage is not only one false claim. It is trust in *who can speak* — newsroom
intake, encyclopedic peers, Zoom hierarchy, school friendship, pastoral robe.
One-example posts should pick a single specimen from the filed traces.
