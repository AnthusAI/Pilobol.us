# Heart meets bot — Chinese teens/adults who would rather talk to AI

- Link: http://www.china.org.cn/2026-08/24/content_118661640.shtml
- Outlet: China.org.cn / Beijing Review (Lu Yan)
- Author: Lu Yan
- Date: 2026-08-24
- Seen: 2026-09-08 (prefer-AI-over-people haul)
- Kind: survey + named admission — prefer AI over people / never judges

Claim: Beijing Anding Hospital: 15-year-old in treatment kept citing a “friend she couldn’t live without” — therapist Xu Gaoyang found it was an AI chatbot, not a classmate. China Youth and Children Research Center survey (Sun Hongyan; n=8,563 students): 35.2% chat/confide with AI; of young AI users, 46.4% would take worries to a chatbot rather than their immediate circle; **21.5% admitted they “would rather talk to AI than to real people.”** Named adult Li Huan (Beijing marketing consultant): ~1 hour/day with AI voice assistant after work — “During the day at the office, I have to deal with all kinds of interpersonal relationships. When I get home, I just don’t have the energy to talk to people anymore. The AI never gets annoyed with me, and it never judges me.” Policy backdrop: July anthropomorphic-AI rules (no virtual partners for minors; 2-hour reminder; ban inducing emotional dependence).
