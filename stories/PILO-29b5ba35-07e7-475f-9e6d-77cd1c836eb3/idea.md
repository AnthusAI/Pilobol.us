# Idea

## Pitch

A 24-year-old said he could see how advanced AI would be built. Silicon Valley treated that clear picture like a product. People handed him a fortune. He borrowed more on top of it. When the market moved against the picture for a few days, the picture had no backup — tens of billions gone in a fire sale the same week he got married. The weird is not the stock chart. It is believing a confident forecast of AI so hard that the forecast becomes the only map, until the map eats the money.

## Audience

Street reader who has watched AI prophets on podcasts; desk collecting fungus where certainty about the future of AI substitutes for seeing.

## Working title

He said he could see it

## Shape

Over one shoulder (reportage about Leopold Aschenbrenner; Atlantic Theo Baker spine).
