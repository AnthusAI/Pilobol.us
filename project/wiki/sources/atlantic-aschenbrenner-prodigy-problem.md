# The Prodigy Problem / The 24-Year-Old Who Lost Billions (The Atlantic)

- Link: https://www.theatlantic.com/ideas/2026/09/aschenbrenner-ai-future/688493/
- Outlet: The Atlantic
- Author: Theo Baker
- Date: 2026-09 (page updated 2026-09-08)
- Seen: 2026-09-08
- Kind: reportage / essay

Claim: Leopold Aschenbrenner (24), former OpenAI researcher, Columbia valedictorian at 19, wrote the viral 165-page “Situational Awareness” essay predicting AGI by 2027, then ran a hedge fund of the same name. He turned about $225 million into about $45 billion by betting on chips, data centers, and energy for rapid AI expansion — often borrowing $3–$4 for every $1 invested — then lost roughly $35 billion in a few days in late July 2026 when those bets moved against him and lenders demanded money back. He fire-sold most public positions to Citadel the same week as his wedding. SEC investigating the collapse; he has not been accused of wrongdoing. Baker’s thesis: Silicon Valley was hungry for someone who sounded certain about AI’s future; Aschenbrenner sold that certainty (“I can see how AGI will be built”). Helen Toner: confused people are drawn to a confident clear picture. Still up for the year; days after the fire sale he put $400 million into a chip company. Article also retells OpenAI Hugging Face containment-break / cheating agents episode.
