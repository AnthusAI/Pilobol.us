# An Analysis of Rumor (Allport & Postman)

- Link: https://www.romolocapuano.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/10/ALLPORT_POSTMAN_AN-ANALYSIS-OF-RUMOR.pdf
- Outlet: Public Opinion Quarterly (Gordon W. Allport & Leo Postman; precursor to *The Psychology of Rumor*, Holt, 1947)
- Date: Winter 1946–47 (book 1947)
- Seen: 2026-09-07
- Kind: classic social psychology (rumor transmission)

Claim: Basic law of rumor: intensity ≈ importance × ambiguity (R ~ i × a) — multiplicative; if either factor is zero, no rumor. Wartime cases show rumor also thickens when news is *plentiful* and momentous (Pearl Harbor report, V-J Day, FDR death), not only when news is absent. Transmission distorts via leveling, sharpening, assimilation to the teller’s interests and stereotypes. Founding lane for “why big events sprout many unverified versions.”
