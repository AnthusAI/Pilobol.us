# Black Mirror unintended consequences

Last updated: 2026-09-08 (Ryan — standing coverage pattern)

House coverage pattern (Ryan 2026-09-08): **Black Mirror–style unintended
consequences of technology**, especially AI / machine learning / related
systems, in real life. Cool or useful tech; then a lived scene where an outcome
nobody sold becomes the story — someone is living inside that outcome, or
glorifying it, or discovering it too late.

Sibling of [prior-failure-amplification](prior-failure-amplification.md),
[institutional-lag](institutional-lag.md), [monitoring-wall](monitoring-wall.md),
[fungus-among-us](fungus-among-us.md). Distinct from the older VOICE line that
only required a “tech bite” for feed posts: this is a **scoutable archetype**,
not only a filter.

Also called: Black Mirror landing, unintended-outcome episode, tech gotcha lived.

## What it is

A real-world scene that feels like a Black Mirror episode: technology that was
supposed to help (or amuse, or optimize) produces a weird, consequential
side-effect that becomes the main event. Prefer AI / ML / platforms / bots /
generative media / robots. The story is the unintended outcome lived by a
person or community — not a policy lecture about risk.

## What it is not

Not a review of the TV show. Not “experts warn about AI.” Not a product launch
with a dark sidebar. Not every bad tech outcome (need a concrete surreal or
uncanny scene). Ordinary hoodwinks stay wiki-only unless they clear the
belief→consequence bar or Historical rescue.

## Scout method (Ryan 2026-09-08) — HARD

Do **not** start by inventing abstract categories and searching those. Start by
finding people who already named the feeling:

- “like Black Mirror”
- “Black Mirror episode”
- “reminds me of Black Mirror”
- “this is so Black Mirror”
- “straight out of Black Mirror”
- similar phrasings

Then open what they are pointing at and evaluate the underlying real-world
incident for Pilobolus (weird story, AI/tech seam, sourceable). File keepers
for strong landings; draft only when the scene clears the reader bar.

## Thesis

The culture already tags these moments. Follow the tag to the specimen; tell
the lived unintended outcome without naming our house or lecturing the show.
