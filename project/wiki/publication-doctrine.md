# Publication doctrine (Slice 1)

Wiki page. Mission lives only at [mission.md](mission.md). Reader voice at [`content/VOICE.md`](../../content/VOICE.md) ([style-guide.md](style-guide.md) is an index). No separate `doctrine/` folder.

**Pilobolus** (site/brand; **Pilobol.us** is the domain-name trick) is a newsroom about a fungus among us: the creeping role and
control of AI over human society — a gradual **strangler-fig** transformation
happening right now (Fowler’s strangler fig — inverted toward humanity). Synthetic interlocutors infecting the discussion circle
until trust collapses are one face of that larger infection.

This is a **Papyrus local pod**: the newsroom desk and its on-disk knowledge
base for Pilobol.us. Desk language stays “Papyrus local pod” even though
**Biblicus** is the underlying knowledge-base system and **Papyrus** is the
automated newsroom that runs on top of it. When agents say “the KB,” they mean
this pod’s wiki/corpus path as Papyrus local mode exposes it — not a separate
product name for day-to-day work.

Reader-facing copy will eventually publish through a Markus (Anthus-flavored
Markdown) front end on Papyrus. Until that path exists, the wiki (mission + concepts) is the source of truth
for what this publication is.

## Mission

Canonical reader-facing statement: [Mission](mission.md) (Ryan 2026-09-06;
revised same day — no self-reference; the talk itself is a buoy of the infection).

Voice rule for mission / about copy: **do not refer to the publication as an
actor** (no “we hunt,” no “Pilobol.us watches”). Impersonal. Weird / Halloween.
**Never talk about Pilobolus in reader stories** (Ryan 2026-09-07) — no branded
“take,” no naming the house; the scene carries the opinion.
Include the twist: this discourse is researched and written by swarms of AI bots
looking for weirdness from AI bots — itself a fruiting body of the fungus.

## What we are not

- Not a generic AI product blog, Anthus company channel, or threat-intel feed.
- Not accelerationist cheerleading or Luddite catastrophism. Name the infection
  without selling a cure-all.
- Not a dump of model releases, benchmark scores, or vendor announcements
  unless they change how discourse itself is faked or trusted.
- Do not invent citations, attribute quotes you have not verified, or pad the
  concept wiki with unverified source pages.

## Standing themes

Treat these as editorial DNA, not one-off beats. New stories should touch at
least one; many will braid several.

1. **Fungus among us / strangler-fig** — AI’s creeping control of human society;
   bots and synthetic speakers as mycelium: distributed, hard to see at the
   edge, structural once inside; the host still looks like itself.
2. **Infiltrated circle** — Harari’s digital public sphere: when you cannot tell
   who is real, the conversation itself loses trust.
3. **Simulacrum** — Baudrillard: copies without originals; maps that precede and
   erase territory; stages of the image until the real is optional.
4. **Believing / confusing the simulacrum** — people **and bots** who confuse
   the copy with reality, or forget it is a copy and live inside it (realer
   than real). Prefer these specimens.
5. **Social media believers** — people who think the feed is all real;
   act on Instagram/TikTok/X as territory (current Ryan focus, 2026-09-06).
6. **Uncanny hyperreality** — uncanny valley + simulacrum more real than real;
   the seam where creepy-almost and seductively-preferable meet. Includes the
   simulacrum social media establishes that it doesn’t want you to see.
7. **Hyperreality × current events** — use the concept on what’s happening now
   (not only theory primers).
8. **Subtle steering** — ML influence people do not realize: feeds shaping a
   simulacrum for engagement; routing/ranking that moves bodies or attention
   while users believe the system is merely helping them.
9. **Counterfeiting** — common thread: fake money / fake people / belief-rainbows
   (Dennett→Harari; crypto as naked case; Skittles as illustration). See
   [counterfeiting](concepts/counterfeiting.md).
10. **Historical lineage / On this day** — when a live specimen is on-topic but
   too common for a feed post, dig backward and make the *origin of the type*
   the interesting part; **or** post a past specimen alone (model: War of the
   Worlds 1938). See [historical-lineage](concepts/historical-lineage.md).

11. **Prior failure amplification** — AI accelerates weaknesses humans already
   had (rumor, finished-plot itch, liking the smoother room). Not a zoo of
   hoodwinked humans; the infection making everything increasingly surreal.
   See [prior-failure-amplification](concepts/prior-failure-amplification.md).
12. **Opinionated** — reader posts have a house take (Ryan 2026-09-07). Not tech
   journalism; not a neutral briefing.
13. **Institutional lag / capability disparity** — discordians and malefactors
   farming the gap between attacker speed and defender institutions; new lags
   spawn new attacker classes as tech gets cheaper and everywhere. See
   [institutional-lag](concepts/institutional-lag.md).
14. **Black Mirror unintended consequences** — real-life scenes that feel
   like a Black Mirror episode: cool/useful tech, then an unintended outcome
   lived by someone. Especially AI/ML. Scout by finding people who say a
   thing is “like Black Mirror,” then look at what they mean. See
   [black-mirror-unintended](concepts/black-mirror-unintended.md).


## One example per post

Each published piece centers **one** simple example, explained accessibly and
engagingly. Sister specimens get their own posts — do not braid cages.

## Weird and surreal (hard preference)

**Uncanny hyperreality** (Ryan, 2026-09-05): the **uncanny valley** plus the
**simulacrum being more real than real**. A whole genre — a feeling, a vibe.
That seam is home turf. See `project/wiki/concepts/uncanny-hyperreality.md`.


The whole point of Pilobol.us is to be **weird and surreal**. Hyperreality is
not a policy brief — it is uncanny. When collecting for the knowledge base or
choosing story angles, prefer strange, on-topic specimens over sober
democracy/tech explainers.

**Belief-makes-it-real (Ryan 2026-09-06):** On-topic when something unreal becomes consequential *because* people treat the copy as real. That idea is now **common** — reserve reader posts for **really significant or unusual** examples (scale of harm, uncanny specificity, novel mechanism). Ordinary “they believed the fake” beats stay as wiki keepers — **or** become **Historical** posts by tracing the type’s origin backward through history (Ryan 2026-09-06). See [`content/VOICE.md`](../../content/VOICE.md) and [historical-lineage](concepts/historical-lineage.md).

**Opinionated (Ryan 2026-09-07):** pieces carry a house take — not “experts say,” not a policy explainer. Opinion rides inside the weird story.

**Storytelling (reader posts):** Weird stories rooted in belief — ordinary start, uncanny tilt. **Not tech journalism** (link sources naturally in the sentences they support; no bibliography). Rotate **story shapes** from [`content/VOICE.md`](../../content/VOICE.md): inside the user; over one shoulder; witness; conversation-as-story; arrival; morning after. **Historical** is a post *type* (lineage dig), not a seventh camera angle — it can use any shape. First-person inside-user is only one option. Name the thing in plain English early. Street-readable; no “not A, it’s B”; don’t clone the Father Justin spine unless the scene is unmistakably different. Screenshots and concrete beats when available.

Strong yes: hologram funerals, griefbots / generative ghosts, dead celebrities
touroured as product, AI influencers that deny being AI, Meta’s own fake people,
dead-internet folklore that turned out half-true, **people who believe the
simulacrum**, anything where the copy wins and the scene feels like a dream
you can’t wake from.

Weak default: generic “bots threaten democracy” explainers, vendor white
papers, benchmark news — only keep them when they unlock a stranger tell.

If two sources are equally on-topic, pick the weirder one.

## Voice

- **Impersonal weird** (Ryan 2026-09-06): Halloween / reportage; specimen speaks;
  no publication “we.” Full rules: [`content/VOICE.md`](../../content/VOICE.md)
  ([style-guide.md](style-guide.md) is a short index).
- The site is a zoo of the surreal — prose and presentation should feel uncanny,
  not only the topics. Self-frame: AI slop growing like fungus; optional poke
  that it looks like a bonsai from the pruning chair.
- Prefer metaphor that earns its keep (fungus, circle, copy, bonsai-misread)
  over jargon stacks.
- Internal research and reports stay private. Only the published article is
  reader-facing.
