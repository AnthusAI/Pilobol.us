# Idea

## Pitch

Synthetic sightings of the missing: AI-fabricated “breakthroughs” (kidnapping stills, reunions, bloodied toys) that hijack a live missing-person search. The feed invents a scene the police never found; people believe it hard enough that hope, tips, and trauma move. Touches believing-the-simulacrum, uncanny hyperreality × current events, counterfeit persons/places, liar’s dividend (real searchers drown in copy).

## Audience

Street reader who has shared a missing-child post; desk collecting fungus traces of belief→consequence.

## Working title

The kidnapping that only happened in the feed
