# How Ambient AI Scribes Impact Medical Students

- Link: https://medicine.yale.edu/news-article/ambient-ai-scribes-impact-medical-students/
- Outlet: Yale School of Medicine
- Author: Serena Crawford (Q&A with Jaideep Talwalkar, MD)
- Date: 2026-08-18
- Seen: 2026-09-07 (monitoring-wall haul)
- Kind: university Q&A / education research (agency hollow-out)

Claim: Yale’s Talwalkar describes ambient AI scribes rewriting clinical note practice. Guardrail study: students write their own note first, then see the AI note, then submit a hybrid — no quality drop. Reverse order (AI note first) degrades assessment and plan — the clinical-reasoning sections — “they may not think as much.” Team is building an educational scribe that withholds assessment/plan so trainees cannot prefer the outline over the host. Lived HITL failure mode: human-in-the-loop becomes optional once the draft arrives first.
