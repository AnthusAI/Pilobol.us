# Machine stylometry names Q: Paul Furber and Ron Watkins

- Link: https://www.orphanalytics.com/en/news/whitepaper202201
- Outlet: OrphAnalytics white paper; reported by NYT (Feb 2022), Engadget, Seattle Times
- Date: 2022-02
- Seen: 2026-09-09
- Kind: news / algorithmic unmasking

Claim: Two independent forensic-linguistics teams — OrphAnalytics in Switzerland (three-character sequence patterns) and a French team led by Florian Cafiero and Jean-Baptiste Camps (trained model) — separately analyzed the Q drops and landed on the same two men: South African software developer Paul Furber for the early posts, and Ron Watkins for the later ones after the account moved to 8chan. The French team's software recognized Watkins' writing in 99% of tests and Furber's in 98%. Furber denied it.

House value: the most consequential anonymous account in modern American life, named by software that never left the office. Machine-made unmasking, pre-LLM, of a real named person — the hinge between human-detective unmaskings and cheap agent pipelines.
