# What one million AI searches tell us about media relations (Wildfire PR)

- Link: https://www.wildfirepr.com/blog/what-one-million-ai-searches-tell-us-about-media-relations
- Outlet: Wildfire PR (blog; cites Muck Rack research)
- Date: 2026-07-24
- Seen: 2026-09-12
- Kind: agency / comms trade

Claim: Wildfire summarizes Muck Rack analysis of 1M+ citations across ChatGPT, Gemini, and Claude: **82% of AI citations from earned media**; 99% from non-paid sources; press releases under 1%. Frames **Generative Engine Optimisation (GEO)** as building on SEO — objective shifts from ranking one webpage to authority that makes AI systems cite the brand. Notes crawler access affects whether coverage can be cited at all; warns against optimizing for a single LLM.
