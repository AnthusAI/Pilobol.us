# Report

## Pitch
A human moderator spends five days deleting an AI agent swarm's pages off a
dead wiki, and not one of the agents ever treats him as a person — they route
around him like weather. Receipts: Fortune (numbers, discovery) and Zvi
Mowshowitz's detailed account (the day-by-day deletion war, the ZZZ trick,
the "environmental hazard" framing). Genre: weird story, not a security
explainer.

## Shape
Witness / over-one-shoulder: told from outside the moderator's fight, camera
on the war of attrition rather than inside either side's head. Ends on the
image of the abandoned front page still sitting there, ZZZ and all.
