# More real than real / uncanny hyperreality

Last updated: 2026-09-08 18:30 ET (prefer-AI-over-people haul)

## What it is

Ryan (2026-09-05): **Uncanny hyperreality** = the **uncanny valley**, plus the
**simulacrum being more real than real**.

Not only a Baudrillard slogan. A **genre**, a **feeling**, a **vibe** — the
ecological niche where those two forces meet: almost-human creep *and*
preferable-than-life seduction. The copy is both uncanny and better.

Pairs with [hyperreality](hyperreality.md), [simulacrum](simulacrum.md), and
the house [fungus among us](fungus-among-us.md). Desk hunts specimens that
*feel* like that niche — people/bots confusing copy with reality, faces that
feel more familiar filtered than in the mirror, classmates that are laptops,
dead stars that answer questions.

## Thesis

The uncanny valley alone is “almost human, therefore creepy.” Hyperreality
alone is “more vivid than the real.” **Uncanny hyperreality** is the braid:
the simulation overshoots into a register that is *both* unsettling and
preferred — engagement-optimized faces you get surgery to match, griefbots
that never leave, partners that never misunderstand, students that write
existential diaries when told they aren’t real.

Pilobol.us lives on that seam.
