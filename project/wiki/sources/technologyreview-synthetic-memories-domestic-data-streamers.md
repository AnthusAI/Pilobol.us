# Generative AI can turn your most precious memories into photos that never existed (MIT Technology Review)

- Link: https://www.technologyreview.com/2024/04/10/1091053/generative-ai-turn-your-most-precious-memories-into-photos/
- Outlet: MIT Technology Review
- Date: 2024-04-10
- Seen: 2026-09-08
- Kind: feature / project specimen

Claim: Domestic Data Streamers’ **Synthetic Memories** (Barcelona; UN/Google funding) interviews people, then prompt-engineers DALL·E / Stable Diffusion to generate memory-based reconstructions of scenes never photographed or photos lost in migration. Subjects have visceral “Oh! Yes! It was like that!” clicks. Work with immigrant/refugee communities and care-home reminiscence therapy (dementia pilot → Toronto clinical study planned). Blurry older models preferred — photoreal Midjourney made people fixate on invented details; “glitches are a feature” because memories behave like dreams. Filmmaker Sam Lawton’s *Expanded Childhood* extends family photos; his father: “Something’s wrong… Do I just not remember it?” Garcia insists synthetic must stay visually distinct from photography — fears OpenAI retiring DALL·E 2.
