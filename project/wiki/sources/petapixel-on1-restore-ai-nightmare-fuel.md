# ON1 Restore AI Turns Old Family Photos into Grotesque Nightmare Fuel (PetaPixel)

- Date: 2026-03-25
- Seen: 2026-09-08
- Kind: product review / surreal specimen

Claim: ON1’s Restore AI (Photo RAW MAX) press kit ships ~73 “restored” examples that redraw rather than repair. Faces get makeup, added hair, “attractive” feature enhancement; ages and identities shift so family members become unrecognizable. Smaller/blurrier faces go grotesque — sharp inhuman lines, messy eyes, terrible smiles; vector-esque early-Firefly look. Company admits mix of open-source + in-house models; face restoration applied universally in early builds; tiny faces “better to just turn it off.” Cloud-only large models; perpetual license gets one year of restores. Marketing: recover family history; output: preferred strangers and nightmare kin.
