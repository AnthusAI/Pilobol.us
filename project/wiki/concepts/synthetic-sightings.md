## What it is

Not a private griefbot. The copy enters the **public search**: a kidnapping
still, a “miracle reunion,” a bloodied toy the police never found. Engagement
farms and answer-engines mint scenes that look like leads. Families and
searchers treat the feed as territory.

## Thesis

The uncanny move is inventing what the ground refused to give. When people
share the fake breakthrough, the simulacrum becomes a second investigation —
one that can drown the first.
