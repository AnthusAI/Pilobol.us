# Conspiracies, Ideological Entrepreneurs, and Digital Popular Culture (Hyzen & Van den Bulck)

Source: https://www.cogitatiopress.com/mediaandcommunication/article/viewFile/4092/2268

## Claim (from Pilobol.us wiki keeper)

Conspiracy theories move margins→mainstream via **ideological entrepreneurs** (North/Storr lineage) who sell alternative world-explanations in upheaval, using digital-media and popular-culture affordances plus fandom-like audiences. Cases: Alex Jones / Infowars and QAnon. Complements Kuran/Sunstein’s “availability entrepreneurs” with a propaganda + celebrity + participatory-culture frame for who *seeds* competing event narratives.
