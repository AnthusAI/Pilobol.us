# Situational Awareness: The Decade Ahead (primary essay)

- Link: https://situational-awareness.ai/
- Outlet: situational-awareness.ai (self-published monograph / essay series)
- Author: Leopold Aschenbrenner
- Date: 2024-06
- Seen: 2026-09-08 (riding-high haul)
- Kind: primary prophecy / manifesto (165-page series; PDF available on site)

Claim: Aschenbrenner’s own “clear picture.” Opening: “You can see the future first in San Francisco.” AGI race has begun; by 2025/26 machines outpace many college graduates; by end of decade, superintelligence. “Everyone is now talking about AI, but few have the faintest glimmer of what is about to hit them.” Claims “perhaps a few hundred people, most of them in San Francisco and the AI labs, that have situational awareness. Through whatever peculiar forces of fate, I have found myself amongst them.” Trendlines: GPT-2→GPT-4 as preschooler→smart high-schooler in ~4 years; another such jump by 2027 via compute (~0.5 OOM/yr), algorithms (~0.5 OOM/yr), and “unhobbling” (chatbot→agent). Then intelligence explosion: hundreds of millions of AGIs automate AI research. Trillion-dollar clusters, lab lockdown vs CCP theft, superalignment, free-world race, and “The Project” — USG AGI project by ~27/28. Dedicated to Ilya Sutskever. This is the document that became required reading, the fund’s name, and the investable prophecy Silicon Valley priced as real.
