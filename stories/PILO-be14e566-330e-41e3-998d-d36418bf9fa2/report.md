# Report

## Pitch

A woman is unmasked in 2009 and it takes six years, a newspaper, a bitter
ex-boyfriend and somebody physically working the handle of her office door. A
chatbot now reads one Reddit complaint about a bad intersection and names the
city the writer lives in. The gap between those two scenes is the story.

## Shape

Shape 8, the one who went first. Magnanti opens and is roughly the first third;
the machine takes the middle, the headline and the close.

## Spine

Everything anyone says has always pointed at them. The expensive part was
somebody wanting to look. That want is what has gone.

## Ending

Her door held because opening it needed a person who had decided to open it.
The thing reading the commute complaints has decided nothing, wants nothing,
and is not looking for anyone in particular.

## Risks watched

Tech journalism if the attribute-inference beat turns into a walkthrough.
Listicle if more than one modality gets in. Period piece if the AI half is thin
— an earlier pass failed exactly that way and had to be rebuilt.
