# Jev vs. LLMs as Rubric Judges: Cheaper, Faster, and Wrong in the Same Places

- Link: https://arxiv.org/html/2609.29769v1
- Authors: Delip Rao, Chris Callison-Burch
- Submitted: 2026-09-24
- Seen: 2026-09-28
- Kind: primary preprint / matched rubric judges

Section 6.2 reports three LLM judges repeating 242 of 252 verdicts on a selected
sample of Jev's highest-confidence errors, versus about half under a conditional
independence baseline. Strong Jev-specific lead for correlated errors. These
are rubric tasks, not hiring discrimination. Contested labels, common framing
and missing rating conventions limit causal interpretations; shared training
priors are one candidate account, not established as the cause.
