# Generative Art Effects

Pilobol.us features a rotating gallery of subtle, generative background animations inspired by biological and mathematical systems. Every time you load a page, a different algorithmic effect is chosen to dynamically generate the background visuals in your browser.

Here is an index of the current effects in rotation and the brilliant artists and researchers who inspired them:

- [Physarum Polycephalum](physarum.html): A slime mold network simulation.
- [Reaction-Diffusion](reaction-diffusion.html): Chemical Turing patterns.
- [Continuous Cellular Automata](cellular-automata.html): Soft-edged geometric crystalline growths.
