# An Analysis of Rumor (Allport & Postman)

Source: https://www.romolocapuano.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/10/ALLPORT_POSTMAN_AN-ANALYSIS-OF-RUMOR.pdf

## Claim (from Pilobol.us wiki keeper)

Basic law of rumor: intensity ≈ importance × ambiguity (R ~ i × a) — multiplicative; if either factor is zero, no rumor. Wartime cases show rumor also thickens when news is *plentiful* and momentous (Pearl Harbor report, V-J Day, FDR death), not only when news is absent. Transmission distorts via leveling, sharpening, assimilation to the teller’s interests and stereotypes. Founding lane for “why big events sprout many unverified versions.”
