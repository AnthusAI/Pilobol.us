# Hyperreality

Last updated: 2026-09-07 09:20 ET

## What it is

Baudrillard’s name for the condition where simulations don’t just fake the real —
they become **realer than real**: more vivid, more trusted, more actionable than
whatever they once pointed at. Closely tied to [simulacrum](simulacrum.md)
(the copy without original) and the house metaphor
[fungus among us](fungus-among-us.md) (how the copy colonizes the host).

### Matrix misreading (desk note)

Do not treat *The Matrix* as Baudrillard 101. Staples (NYT Editorial Observer,
2002): the film popularized the book while Baudrillard said Hollywood mostly
misunderstood him — a simulacrum of the theory circulating wider than the theory
([staples-matrix-baudrillard](../sources/staples-matrix-baudrillard.md)). Prefer
Eco’s [travels in hyperreality](travels-in-hyperreality.md) / feed specimens over
red-pill allegory.

## Thesis

Ryan (2026-09-05): also **people and bots who confuse the simulacrum with
reality**, and **hyperreality as a concept as it relates to current events**.


Ryan (2026-09-05): especially interested in stuff about people who **end up
believing the simulacrum**. Also: weird traces of the simulacrum social media
establishes that it doesn’t want people to see — uncanny **realer than real**,
more realistic than actual reality.

Pilobol.us cares about the moment people *forget* they are in the copy — when
the feed, the AI companion, the Instagram place, or the war clip is preferred
to the messier referent. That forgetfulness is editorial gold: not “AI bad,”
but the eclipse of reality by a better-feeling simulation.
