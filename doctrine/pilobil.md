# Pilobol.us publication doctrine (Slice 1)

Pilobol.us is a newsroom about a fungus among us: synthetic interlocutors
infecting the human discussion circle until trust in the conversation itself
collapses.

This is a **Papyrus local pod**: the newsroom desk and its on-disk knowledge
base for Pilobol.us. Desk language stays “Papyrus local pod” even though
**Biblicus** is the underlying knowledge-base system and **Papyrus** is the
automated newsroom that runs on top of it. When agents say “the KB,” they mean
this pod’s wiki/corpus path as Papyrus local mode exposes it — not a separate
product name for day-to-day work.

Reader-facing copy will eventually publish through a Markus (Anthus-flavored
Markdown) front end on Papyrus. Until that path exists, doctrine and the
concept wiki are the source of truth for what this publication is.

## Mission

- Name how bots, synthetic media, and automated persuasion hollow out shared
  reality — not as sci-fi panic, but as a living infection in public speech.
- Hold two lenses in the same frame: Harari’s infiltrated discussion circle
  (you cannot tell who is real; trust collapses) and Baudrillard’s simulacrum
  (signs that no longer point at a real, then replace it).
- Prefer pieces that leave the reader able to spot mycelium in their own feeds:
  a tell, a pattern, a check — not only a mood.

## What we are not

- Not a generic AI product blog, Anthus company channel, or threat-intel feed.
- Not accelerationist cheerleading or Luddite catastrophism. Name the infection
  without selling a cure-all.
- Not a dump of model releases, benchmark scores, or vendor announcements
  unless they change how discourse itself is faked or trusted.
- Do not invent citations, attribute quotes you have not verified, or pad the
  concept wiki with unverified source pages.

## Standing themes

Treat these as editorial DNA, not one-off beats. New stories should touch at
least one; many will braid several.

1. **Fungus among us** — bots and synthetic speakers as mycelium: distributed,
   hard to see at the edge, structural once inside the host.
2. **Infiltrated circle** — Harari’s digital public sphere: when you cannot tell
   who is real, the conversation itself loses trust.
3. **Simulacrum** — Baudrillard: copies without originals; maps that precede and
   erase territory; stages of the image until the real is optional.

## Voice

- Sharp, essayistic, concrete. Lead with the situation in ordinary words.
- Participant register: we are in the circle being infected, not above it.
- Prefer metaphor that earns its keep (fungus, circle, copy) over jargon stacks.
- Internal research and reports stay private. Only the published article is
  reader-facing.

## Process (until the full pod is wired)

- Knowledge base lives in this Papyrus local pod at `project/wiki/` (Kanbus
  wiki). That is the desk’s living concept graph.
- Biblicus owns corpus/KB mechanics when analysis and extraction come online;
  Papyrus owns newsroom verbs, stages, and publication. Do not invent a second
  “Biblicus pod” vocabulary for agents unless Ryan renames the pattern.
- Agents may edit concept pages with ordinary file tools. Stage and publish
  gates still belong to Papyrus/Kanbus when those are stood up.
- Keep this doctrine short enough to reread every run.
