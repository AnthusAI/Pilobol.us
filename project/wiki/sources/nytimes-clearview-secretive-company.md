# The Secretive Company That Might End Privacy as We Know It (NYT)

- Link: https://www.nytimes.com/2020/01/18/technology/clearview-privacy-facial-recognition.html
- Outlet: The New York Times
- Date: 2020-01-18
- Seen: 2026-09-09
- Kind: news (seed for Clearview / practical obscurity)

Claim: Clearview AI built a facial-recognition app for law enforcement: upload a photo of a face, get back public photos of that person plus links to where those photos appeared. Backbone was a scraped database of billions of images from Facebook, YouTube, Venmo, and the open web — without user consent. Early customers included local police, FBI, DHS. Framed as ending the ability to walk down the street anonymously.
