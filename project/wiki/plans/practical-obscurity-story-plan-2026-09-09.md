## Overall narrative arc

1. **Ordinary start** — someone trusts a private or pseudonymous life (secret blog / burner / friends-only), Gen X memory of parties and blogs that stayed obscure because stitching was expensive.
2. **Attribute-inference walkthrough (core teaching beat)** — ordinary scraps assemble into a soft profile (age, job, city, money…). Cover / mid-piece figure = Beyond Memorization / kjam attribute-inference diagram. This is the extract step.
3. **Match / name** — soft profile finds a real life (LinkedIn, paper, named account). Inline link to Lermen–Paleka arXiv (and WSJ as the door people walked through). Classic unmaskings as lineage: what used to need a journalist.
4. **Even when nothing is public** — second-hand privacy: friends’ AI agents; platform-operator distrust still there.
5. **Stack / fusion ending** — quiet chords, not explainers: Clearview (face), DNA/cousin spit kits, Flock (privileged mobility), democratization ($1–4 / stalker desk). State-backed actor who can run them together. End on image/implication, not summary.

Likely shape: **over one shoulder** or **inside the user**, with optional **lineage** glances at classic outs. Opening specimen TBD (Belle de Jour / Fake Steve / composite).
