## Claim (from Pilobol.us wiki keeper)

Confirmed historical false-flag *operations* exist (e.g. Gleiwitz 1939, Mukden 1931) but are outnumbered by baseless claims that *real* crises were staged. After 9/11 and Iraq WMD deception, “false flag” became a reusable interpretive frame (Sandy Hook “crisis actors,” Jan. 6, school shootings).
