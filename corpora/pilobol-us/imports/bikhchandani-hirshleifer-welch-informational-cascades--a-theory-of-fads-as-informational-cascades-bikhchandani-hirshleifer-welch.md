## Claim / why accepted

An **informational cascade** starts when it is optimal for an individual, having seen predecessors’ *actions*, to follow them and ignore private information. Explains localized conformity *and* fragility — small shocks or a little new public information can reverse long-running mass behavior. When only previous actions are observable (not signals), cascades can be wrong yet brittle. Microfoundation under availability/conspiracy cascade talk.
