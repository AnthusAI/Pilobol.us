On a September weekend in 2026, more than ten coaches rolled into Jurilovca, a small town on Romania’s Danube Delta, for the Lipovan borscht festival.

The festival was not happening.

The mayor, Eugen Ion, had not announced one. The town had skipped it. Still the coaches came, and so did cars from across the country, people who had planned a weekend around bowls of soup, music, and a place that knew how to put on a show.

When locals asked how everyone knew to come, some pointed at Facebook and travel-agency pages selling Delta packages with the festival’s name on them. Others said something simpler. They had asked ChatGPT whether the festival was on this weekend. The chat had said yes — this weekend, the fourth and fifth of September.

The mayor tried the same question himself. He got the same wrong answer.

There was soup somewhere in the world that weekend. It was not the festival they had driven for. The coaches had already spent the fuel.
