# Most employees would rather ask ChatGPT for advice than their manager

- Link: https://www.cpapracticeadvisor.com/2025/09/16/most-employees-would-rather-ask-chatgpt-for-advice-than-their-manager-survey-finds/169045/
- Outlet: CPA Practice Advisor (Resume Now “AI Boss Effect” report)
- Author: CPA Practice Advisor staff (Keith Spencer quoted)
- Date: 2025-09-16
- Seen: 2026-09-08 (prefer-AI-over-people haul)
- Kind: workplace survey — prefer AI over human manager for advice/support

Claim: Resume Now survey (~1,000 employees): 97% sought workplace advice from AI instead of their boss; 63% frequently. Reasons framed as safer/faster/judgment-free vs human manager: 57% fear retaliation for asking the wrong question; 47% speed; 38% don’t want to seem incompetent; 32% “feel more comfortable asking AI than a person”; 70% say ChatGPT understands work challenges better than their manager. Outcomes: 72% say ChatGPT gives better advice than boss; 49% say it’s been more emotionally supportive than their manager; 93% would discuss mental health/stress with an AI assistant. Keith Spencer (Resume Now): “When people turn to AI for emotional support or advice, that is not just a tech trend. It is a trust gap.” Preference admission at scale: the copy is the safer interlocutor.
