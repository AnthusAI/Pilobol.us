# Your Permission Leaks My Data: Second-Hand Privacy Harms in LLM Agents

- Link: https://doi.org/10.1145/3772363.3798860
- Outlet: ACM (research)
- Date: ~2025–2026
- Seen: 2026-09-09
- Kind: paper

Claim: Personal LLM agents with broad permissions (mail, messages, calendar, docs) do not only process the authorizing user’s data — they also see sensitive traces about friends, colleagues, and family. Second-hand privacy (2HP): your friend’s grant becomes inference about you without your consent. Exposure depends on which channels are granted; cross-channel access amplifies stitching. Ryan beat (2026-09-09): even “private” social circles now include any AI agents friends authorized, on purpose or by accident — plus lingering distrust of platform operators.
