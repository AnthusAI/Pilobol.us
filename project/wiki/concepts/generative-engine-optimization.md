# Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)

Last updated: 2026-09-12 (board PILO-72bae0)

## What it is

**Generative Engine Optimization** (GEO) and related **AEO** (“answer engine optimization”) name the practice of shaping what **AI answer engines** — ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, Google AI Mode / Overviews — **cite or recommend** when synthesizing a response. Success metrics shift from rank position and click-through to **citation frequency**, share of voice in generated answers, and AI-attributed traffic.

Agency pitch copy often frames GEO as **earned media operationalized**: LLMs cite licensed publications and third-party coverage; PR earns placements; marketing chases schema on owned sites. The loop is **Ouroboros-shaped**: credibility manufactured for the model, then consumed by humans as oracle output.

Parallel lane: **UGC seeding** — spam or covert promo on Reddit and forums so retrieval systems scrape brand-friendly text into answers (see WARP / mod responses in Specimens).

## The loop (Ryan 2026-09-13)

Desk shorthand "Ouroboros" means the **training** loop, not the commercial one:
machine-written text is planted where crawlers graze, is learned by the next
model, and shapes what that model volunteers without retrieving anything. The
industry pitch has moved past citation-today toward presence in the next
training set. Recursive training on machine output is itself documented —
[Shumailov et al., *Nature* 2024](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-024-07566-y)
— as degradation across generations, framed there as accidental contamination.
GEO is the same process performed **on purpose**, with a budget.

Open gap: the training-loop half of this is not yet filed to house standard.
Needs primaries on AI-generated share of the crawl and on deliberate
training-data poisoning; current web sources are SEO vendors and secondary
summaries. Do not put unfiled claims into reader copy.
