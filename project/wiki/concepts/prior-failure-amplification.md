# Prior failure amplification

Last updated: 2026-09-07

House thesis (Ryan 2026-09-07): AI often makes society worse **not by inventing
new ways for humans to fail**, but by **accelerating and amplifying weaknesses
that were already there**. Sibling of [fungus-among-us](fungus-among-us.md) and
[competing-event-narratives](competing-event-narratives.md); braids
[historical-lineage](historical-lineage.md), [subtle-steering](subtle-steering.md),
[information-habit](information-habit.md).

## What it is

Humans already mint competing plots after ambiguous events, already prefer a
finished story to an unfinished one, already treat helpful systems as neutral,
already confuse a convincing copy with the thing it stands for. Platforms and
generative models do not invent those pressures. They **compress the clock**,
**raise the volume**, and **offshore the entrepreneurship** (click farms, synthetic
sightings, availability cascades at machine speed) until the host still looks
like itself — only louder, faster, and stranger.

This is the strangler-fig move: wrap an old weakness until the echo of the human
shape is what remains.

## What it is not

Not a zoo of **hoodwinked humans** — one fooled person after another as the
whole publication. Ordinary “someone believed a fake online” beats are **wiki
keepers**, not the feed’s through-line. The through-line is the **creeping
infection that makes everything increasingly surreal**: the room tilting, the
copy outcompeting life, old failure modes fruiting at industrial scale.

## Thesis

Amplification of **prior** failure modes, not a new species of failure. Reader
posts should show the *infection’s texture* (surreal tilt, mycelial scale,
lineage of the type) — not a ledger of victims who got taken in.
