# GEDmatch and the Golden State Killer (NPR)

- Link: https://www.npr.org/sections/thetwo-way/2018/04/27/606624218/in-hunt-for-golden-state-killer-investigators-uploaded-his-dna-to-genealogy-site
- Outlet: NPR
- Date: 2018-04-27
- Seen: 2026-09-09
- Kind: news / forensic genetic genealogy

Claim: Investigators uploaded crime-scene DNA-derived profiles to GEDmatch, a public genealogy site where people upload consumer DNA to find relatives. Partial matches to distant relatives + traditional family-tree work led to Joseph DeAngelo. Practical obscurity for DNA: your cousin’s hobby spit kit can point at you.
