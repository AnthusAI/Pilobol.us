# Do AI-Restored Photos Change Too Much? (BestPhoto Blog)

Claim: Canonical r/photorestoration complaint: “Remini made my grandma look like a stranger” — weekly side-by-sides of wonderful real faces vs smoothed approximations. Remini/MyHeritage/VanceAI/GFPGAN stacks trained on modern hi-res faces pull blurry 1960s portraits toward “average young adult”; great-grandfather emerges 40 years younger with ceramic skin. Three failure modes: hallucinated features (invented teeth in closed-mouth smiles; wrong eye color); plastic skin (pores/wrinkles erased → wax figure); inaccurate colorization (green dresses, white wedding gowns by training bias). “Would your family recognize them?” checklist before funeral slideshows. Vendor-promotional frame, but the stranger-grandma texture is on-rubric.
