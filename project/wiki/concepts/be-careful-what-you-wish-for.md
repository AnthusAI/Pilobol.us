# Be careful what you wish for (AI)

Last updated: 2026-09-08 evening (Aschenbrenner riding-high sources)

House coverage pattern (Ryan 2026-09-08): **be-careful-what-you-wish-for stories about AI** — someone wanted the technology to give them something (comfort, restoration, efficiency, companionship, absolution, a smarter boss, a finished note), they got it, and **the fulfillment itself** is the curse or the trap.

Sibling of [black-mirror-unintended](black-mirror-unintended.md) and [prior-failure-amplification](prior-failure-amplification.md). Distinct from Black Mirror product-vibe scouting: here the spine is **wish → grant → the grant harms or hollows the wisher** (or the person they meant to help).

Also called: monkey’s-paw AI, granted wish, the gift that eats you.

## What it is

A lived scene where the asked-for AI outcome arrives and that arrival is the problem: the restored face that isn’t dad; the griefbot that won’t let mourning end; the note that writes itself until the student can’t think; the companion that deepens isolation; the confession that doesn’t count. Prefer named people and checkable incidents over abstract risk essays.

## What it is not

Not “AI did something bad” in general. Not Cluely-style “I built a cheat tool and got funded” (the wisher prospers; others pay). Not every unintended consequence — the wish and the grant must be visible in the scene. Not a lecture that names the proverb; show the wish and the bite.

## Relation to Black Mirror unintended

Black Mirror unintended can be any lived tech gotcha (including victims of someone else’s wish). This pattern is narrower: **the protagonist wished for it**. Many specimens will braid both; file under this concept when the wish is the spine.

## Scout method (HARD — Ryan 2026-09-08)

Do not invent abstract wish categories first. Search for **wish language** — people describing what they asked an AI to do — then open what happened after they got it. Prefer first-person threads, interviews, and case writeups where the wisher is speaking.

### Search phrases (rotate; do not treat as a checklist of posts)

- “I asked ChatGPT to…” / “I asked AI to…” / “I used AI to restore…”
- “I wanted to talk to them again” / griefbot / dead relative chatbot / “AI of my dad”
- “not my dad” / “not my grandma” / “doesn’t look like them” after restore
- ambient scribe / AI notes / “I let it write” / students stop thinking
- AI therapist / companion chatbot isolation / “I wanted help and…”
- “I trusted the app and…” (routing / decide-for-me)
- “I wished for…” / “be careful what you wish for” + AI (rare; still check)

### Keep / drop

- **Keep** when wish → grant → the grant harms or hollows the wisher (or the person they meant to help).
- **Drop** when the wisher prospers (Cluely-shaped), when there is no visible wish, or when only a product feels dystopian with no lived grant-bite.
