# Doctors vs. Algorithms: Physicians, too, struggle to learn from evidence that contradicts AI suggestions

- Link: https://journals.plos.org/digitalhealth/article?id=10.1371%2Fjournal.pdig.0001490
- Outlet: PLOS Digital Health
- Author: Aranzazu Vinas, Fernando Blanco, Helena Matute
- Date: 2026-07-09
- Seen: 2026-09-07 (monitoring-wall round 3)
- Kind: peer-reviewed experiment (agency hollow-out)

Claim: Two experiments with professional physicians (N=105 / N=118): an AI incorrectly labels fictitious patients as highly vs lowly sensitive to a treatment; doctors see per-patient outcomes that prove the labels wrong. They still dose and rate effectiveness by the AI label. Experiment 2: treatment is completely useless, yet doctors rate it as working—especially for “highly sensitive” patients. EU AI Act–style human-in-the-loop assumption fails under the simplest feedback loop. Distinct from Yale ambient-scribe note-order and Ars radiologist black-box veto.
