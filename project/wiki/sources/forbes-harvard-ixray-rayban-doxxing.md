# I-XRAY: Harvard students dox strangers in real time with Ray-Ban Meta glasses

- Link: https://www.forbes.com/sites/johnkoetsier/2024/10/03/metas-ray-ban-smart-glasses-used-to-instantly-dox-strangers-in-public-thanks-to-ai-and-facial-recognition/
- Outlet: Forbes; also Harvard Crimson
- Authors of the project: AnhPhu Nguyen, Caine Ardayfio
- Date: 2024-10
- Seen: 2026-09-09
- Kind: news / demonstrated unmasking on real strangers

Claim: Two Harvard students wired Ray-Ban Meta glasses to a face-search and public-records pipeline: stream video, detect a face, find more photos of that person online, then pull name, phone number, home address and relatives' names from articles and voter registration databases. They tested it on strangers in public. They approached a woman affiliated with the Cambridge Community Foundation claiming they had met her there and she shook Ardayfio's hand; they approached a man working in minority rights in India and gained his trust; they told a student her home address in Atlanta and her parents' names, and she confirmed they were right. They did not release the tool, saying the point was to show it could be built from parts that already exist.

House value: the scene where it lands on an ordinary person on a sidewalk — a handshake given to a stranger who is reading your life off your face while you talk. Real, dated, named builders, confirmed hits.
