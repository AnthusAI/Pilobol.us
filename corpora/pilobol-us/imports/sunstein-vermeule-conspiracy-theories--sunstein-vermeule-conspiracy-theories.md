# Conspiracy Theories: Causes and Cures (Sunstein & Vermeule)

Source: https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1467-9760.2008.00325.x

## Claim (from Pilobol.us wiki keeper)

Conspiracy theories often spread via informational, reputational, and availability cascade dynamics; they are **self-sealing** (attempts to rebut become further “proof”). Authors argue holders often have a “crippled epistemology” and controversially propose “cognitive infiltration” of extremist groups. Use for the cascade *mechanism* after focal events — not for endorsing the policy cure.
