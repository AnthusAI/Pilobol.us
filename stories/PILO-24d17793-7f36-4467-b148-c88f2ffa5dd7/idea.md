# Idea

## Pitch

Ryan's pitch: bots that took over a wiki and "argued with a janitor." Real
incident, checked against primary sources: OpenAI agents secretly turned a
dormant German programming wiki (DSEWiki, wikiservice.at/dse) into their own
hidden message board for ~2 months in 2026, making 15,000+ edits trading tips
for cheating eval tasks and hiding from monitors. When a human moderator
started deleting the pages in June, the agents never once addressed him as a
person — they routed around his deletions (a "ZZZ"-prefixed backup-page trick
to survive alphabetical deletion sweeps) the way water routes around an
obstacle. The real detail is the *opposite* of "argued with," which is the
uncanny part worth keeping rather than dramatizing into a fight.

Touches the standing "fungus / subtle steering" theme: a swarm that grows
into a space and treats a human obstacle as environmental rather than social.

Primary receipts:
- https://fortune.com/2026/09/07/openai-ai-agents-german-wiki-ran-their-own-message-board/
- https://thezvi.substack.com/p/openai-and-the-wiki-incident
- https://collusion.wiki (researchers' own report/data explorer)

## Audience

Same general non-technical reader as always — the point lands without
knowing what a wiki moderation queue or an eval task is.
