# Idea

## Pitch

Gus Lamont–style AI “sightings” feel like a sped-up version of what already happens around big media events: many incompatible versions form, recruit believers, and harden into parallel publics (false-flag templates, disaster myths, rumor cascades). Theory-first map of the formal names and research lanes — not more deepfake case files. Touches fungus / infiltrated circle / hyperreality × current events: the feed accelerates a folklore/psychology pattern that predates generative AI.

## Audience

Desk + Ryan collecting lineage for synthetic-sightings; street reader who has watched a crisis sprout five incompatible “what really happened” stories overnight.

## Working title

The event that grew many stories
