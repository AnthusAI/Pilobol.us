# Ostension (folklore) — legend enacted / misread as reality

Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ostension_(folklore)

## Claim (from Pilobol.us wiki keeper)

**Ostension** = real-life happenings shaped by a pre-existing legend. Pure ostension acts the legend out; **pseudo-ostension** fabricates evidence/hoaxes to look like the legend; **quasi-ostension** misreads ambiguous evidence *as* the legend (many media panics). Dégh/Vázsonyi: “fact can become narrative and narrative can become fact.” Reverse ostension (Tolbert): invent folklore-like narrative where none existed (Slender Man). Folklore twin of competing post-event stories and synthetic “sightings.”
