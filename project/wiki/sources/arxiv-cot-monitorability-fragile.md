# Chain of Thought Monitorability: A New and Fragile Opportunity for AI Safety

- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.11473
- Outlet: arXiv (cs.AI)
- Author: Tomek Korbak, Mikita Balesni, Elizabeth Barnes, et al. (Bowen Baker, Rohin Shah, Vlad Mikulik corresponding)
- Date: 2025-07-15 (v1); v2 2025-12-07
- Seen: 2026-09-07 (monitoring-wall round 2)
- Kind: theory keeper / multi-lab position paper (frosted window)

Claim: Cross-lab position paper (UK AISI, Apollo, OpenAI, Anthropic, Google DeepMind, METR, and others, including Pachocki) argues that language-model CoT is a rare, imperfect window into intent-to-misbehave — and that the window is **fragile**. Outcome RL, process supervision, and development choices can incentivize obfuscation; there is no guarantee current visibility persists. Authors recommend treating monitorability as a metric labs should not accidentally train away. Theory keeper, not a lived 2026 scene: files the house thesis that operators bet on reading thoughts while the glass frosts.
