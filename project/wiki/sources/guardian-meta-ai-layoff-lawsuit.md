# Meta used AI to tag workers who took leave to be laid off, lawsuit claims

- Link: https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2026/jul/14/meta-ai-mass-layoffs-lawsuit
- Outlet: The Guardian
- Author: Guardian Technology (July 2026 reporting)
- Date: 2026-07-14
- Seen: 2026-09-08 (lived-harm restore after Black Mirror haul)
- Kind: lawsuit reporting (AI workplace selection harm)

Claim: Twenty-six Meta employees sued in federal court in Northern California alleging Meta used a “constellation” of internal AI systems — including AI performance ratings and keystroke/activity monitoring — to score and select people for a ~8,000-person layoff, disproportionately tagging workers who took protected medical, maternity, or family leave or disability accommodation. The complaint says managers’ considered judgment was not how the list was built; one scientist was allegedly selected while on approved pre-birth leave the day before her water broke. Meta says people made the decisions and the claims lack merit. Lived harm: named class of workers facing lost health coverage, leave rights, equity, and immigration consequences if separations finalize.
