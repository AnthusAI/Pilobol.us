## Center of gravity

Pilobol.us is about a **fungus among us**: weird/surreal outcomes of society
slowly taken over by AI/ML algorithms and systems — creeping **strangler-fig**
infection. *Pilobolus*: site/brand name (Pilobol.us = domain trick); also a psychedelic mushroom that grows on shit. Get it?
Mission: `project/wiki/mission.md`. Social media is a rich Baudrillard stage, not
the whole host. Hunt subtle signs and symptoms of the surreal fungal takeover.
**Prior failure amplification** + **opinionated** house take (Ryan 2026-09-07):
not a series of hoodwinked humans; amplify old weaknesses; pieces have a spine.
Lenses: Harari’s infiltrated circle + Baudrillard’s simulacrum / **hyperreality**
(realer than real).

**HARD preference: weird and surreal.** The whole point is uncanny. Prefer
strange on-topic specimens over sober democracy/tech explainers. If two finds
are equally on-topic, pick the weirder one.

**More real than real** is a whole genre: a feeling, a vibe — an ecological
niche within the uncanny valley. Hunt specimens that *feel* like that niche,
not only articles that name Baudrillard.

trace and files it; it does not ship articles.

### Especially: believing / confusing the simulacrum

Strongest keepers are cases where **people and bots confuse the simulacrum with reality** — or people **end up believing the copy** — treat the
AI partner as more real than humans, the hologram as the farewell, the griefbot
as Dad, the synthetic news host as journalism, the filter-self as the self.
Not “someone pointed out a deepfake,” but the moment the simulation *won*.
### Also: hyperreality × current events

**Hyperreality as a concept applied to current events** — news, politics, war
feeds, platform scandals, grief-tech, influencer culture — where the simulation
frame explains what’s happening better than “misinformation” alone.

### Also: the uncanny realer-than-real feed

Anything weird about the simulacrum **social media establishes** that it does not
want people to see — because it is *more realistic than actual reality*,
uncanny realer-than-real. The polished world that outcompetes the messy one;
the engagement surface that feels truer than life; tells and leaks that expose
how the copy was built to be preferred. Prefer specimens where the uncanniness
shows (too perfect, too smooth, wrong in a dreamlike way), not policy PDFs.
