# Pair narrowly escape death after driving off incomplete highway while following Google Maps (AsiaOne)

- Link: https://www.asiaone.com/asia/pair-narrowly-escape-death-after-driving-incomplete-highway-indonesia-while-following-google
- Outlet: AsiaOne (citing Kompas / NZ Herald)
- Author: Koh Xing Ying
- Date: 2025-04-15 (incident 2025-04-05)
- Seen: 2026-09-08 (be-careful-what-you-wish-for haul)
- Kind: named lived Maps wish → body-moved bite

Claim: Rudie Heru Komandono, 61, sets Google Maps toward a destination (friend’s house / navigation help) on the unfinished Krian–Gresik toll near Gresik, East Java. Wish: the app will get him there. Grant: turn-by-turn trust. Bite: too focused on the phone, he misses barriers and incomplete-road signs, drives through a gap in a concrete barrier onto an unconnected elevated stretch, and the BMW plunges ~5–12 m onto a lower highway with passenger Endang Sri Wahyuni; both survive with minor injuries. Local police later stress driver error alongside the Maps story — still a clean drove-to-the-pin adjacent: asked the helpful system for a route and the grant moved the body off a cliff of asphalt.
