# AI won’t replace radiologists, but it will dramatically change their jobs

- Link: https://arstechnica.com/health/2026/08/ai-wont-replace-radiologists-but-it-will-dramatically-change-their-jobs/
- Outlet: Ars Technica (Knowable Magazine)
- Author: Lola Butcher
- Date: 2026-08-25
- Seen: 2026-09-07 (monitoring-wall round 2)
- Kind: feature journalism (agency hollow-out / HITL as veto)

Claim: Distinct from Yale’s note-first scribes: radiology is the densest AI clinic (~3/4 of ~1,400 FDA-cleared AI medical devices by early 2026). Neural-net readers are black boxes; the human job becomes spotting the rare miss in a stream of correct machine calls — “a whole mental rewiring” (Paul Yi, St. Jude). Charles Kahn (RSNA *Radiology: Artificial Intelligence*): is that a real abnormality or “AI with its subtle mind”? Automation bias / complacency: a cited mammography study found even experienced readers’ accuracy collapsed under incorrect AI; Mosaic’s Nina Kottler: if a rad accepts AI 99/100 times when the tool is 95% accurate, “we go talk to that rad.” 2026 AMA survey: more than a quarter of physicians had received no AI training. HITL is still on the form; the loop is already a confidence-calibration problem the host is losing.
