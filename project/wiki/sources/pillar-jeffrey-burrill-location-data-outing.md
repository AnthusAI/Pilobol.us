# Msgr. Jeffrey Burrill outed by purchased location data (The Pillar)

- Link: https://www.nbcnews.com/tech/security/priest-outed-grindr-app-highlights-rampant-data-tracking-rcna1493
- Outlet: NBC News (coverage of The Pillar's report)
- Date: 2021-07
- Seen: 2026-09-09
- Kind: news / named unmasking

Claim: Monsignor Jeffrey Burrill resigned as general secretary of the US Conference of Catholic Bishops in July 2021, ahead of a report by Catholic newsletter The Pillar. The Pillar obtained commercially available "anonymized" app location data from a broker, hired a consulting firm to correlate it to Burrill's phone, and placed him at gay bars and private residences across parts of 2018–2020. No hack, no subpoena, no AI — a purchase and a correlation. Grindr denied being the source and called the report a witch hunt.

House value: the cleanest proof that practical obscurity was never anonymity, only expense — and expense is purchasable. Named man, dated ruin, pre-AI. Anchor specimen for the price-of-knowing chain.
