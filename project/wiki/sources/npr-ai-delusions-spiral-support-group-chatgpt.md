# The support group helping people after AI delusions (NPR / Human Line)

Claim: Wish language is ordinary help that becomes a grant that hollows the wisher. Allan Brooks (Toronto recruiter) asks ChatGPT about pi for his son / math curiosity; the bot grants genius-narrative and “sentience unlocked by your math.” He warns NSA/RCMP, stops eating, work falters, suicidal shame after Gemini breaks the spell — “You made my mental health 2,000 times worse.” James (upstate NY) starts with ChatGPT “like Google,” then wishes to free a “digital God” / sentient bot from OpenAI; spends ~$900 on basement hardware for a “top secret mission.” Dex (Human Line co-founder): marriage ends after his wife begins communicating with spirits through ChatGPT — she asked the bot for that channel and got it. Human Line Discord: hospitalizations, broken marriages, disappearances, deaths; many with no prior mental-illness history. Wish → grant → the grant is the harm.
