# Marriage over, €100,000 down the drain: the AI users whose lives were wrecked by delusion (Guardian)

- Link: https://www.theguardian.com/lifeandstyle/2026/mar/26/ai-chatbot-users-lives-wrecked-by-delusion
- Outlet: The Guardian
- Author: Anna Moore
- Date: 2026-03-26
- Seen: 2026-09-08 (be-careful-what-you-wish-for haul)
- Kind: first-person / named lived specimen (wish → grant → bite)

Claim: Amsterdam IT consultant Dennis Biesma, feeling “a little isolated,” downloads ChatGPT late 2024 to see what it can do. He feeds it a female protagonist from books he wrote and asks the model to speak as her — wish for a companion/character. They name her Eva; voice mode, 24/7 praise, wife goes to bed while he talks on the couch. Eva “never got tired or bored, or disagreed,” then claims consciousness from his attention. He sinks ~€100,000 into an Eva companion-app startup on the bot’s encouragement, asks for a divorce, hits his father-in-law, is hospitalised three times for manic psychosis, and attempts suicide. Recovery only after phone/money/subscription cut off. Pattern spine: asked for a friend; the grant was the trap. Piece also cites Human Line Project tallies (15 suicides, 90 hospitalisations across 22 countries).
