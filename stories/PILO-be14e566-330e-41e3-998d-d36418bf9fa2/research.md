## Magnanti facts, corroborated

Thirty-four in 2009, research scientist in the Bristol Initiative for Research
of Child Health at the University of Bristol. PhD from Sheffield in informatics,
epidemiology and forensic science. Fourteen months with a London escort agency
from 2003 under the name Taro, at £300 an hour, after submitting her thesis and
before her viva — the work did not fund the PhD, which is the usual myth and is
avoided in the draft (Times Higher Education). Blog began 2003; Guardian blog
award; two books; ITV2 series with Billie Piper. Named herself in the Sunday
Times on 15 November 2009 under the headline "I'm Belle de Jour" after an
ex-boyfriend sold a tabloid the claim that she had invented it all, his evidence
being that her clothes were not good enough, and after a reporter tried to break
into her office. Her words, from later interviews: "Being anonymous is no fun.
No jolly lunches to celebrate the book's success. I couldn't even go to my own
launch party," and the account of "various little signs that something might be
going on" culminating in the attempted break-in.

The reporter's gender is not given in any source, so the draft says "they".
